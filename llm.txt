## Core Product Spectrum
1. **Flange Bolts (DIN 6921 / ISO 4162)**: Integrated washer face distributing clamp force over large surface area; available in standard hex, serrated anti-vibration, and heavy industrial finishes.
2. **Hex Bolts (DIN 931 / DIN 933 / ISO 4014 / ISO 4017)**: High-tensile partial and full-thread bolts in Class 8.8, 10.9, 12.9, and stainless steel.
3. **Hex Nuts (DIN 934 / ISO 4032 / DIN 6923 / DIN 985)**: Precision threaded matching nuts, serrated flange nuts, and nylon-insert lock nuts (nyloc).
4. **Screws (DIN 7985 / ISO 7045 / DIN 912)**: Pan head, countersunk, and socket head cap screws for precision electronics, enclosures, and mechanical assemblies.
5. **SEM Screws (DIN 6900 / ISO 10644)**: Pre-assembled screw and washer combinations preventing loose washers and speeding up assembly lines.
6. **Washers (DIN 125 / ISO 7089 / DIN 9021 / DIN 6798)**: Flat form A washers, wide-flange fender washers, and serrated external/internal tooth lock washers.
