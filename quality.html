GLOBAL BENCHMARKS

WIDELY USED INDUSTRIAL STANDARDS.

Industrial fastening applications depend on internationally recognized specifications for dimensional tolerance, tensile strength, and metallurgical integrity. Product requirements may reference applicable standards based on manufacturer specifications.

DIN GERMANY / EUROPE

Deutsches Institut für Normung

Widely adopted metric fastener standards across automotive, industrial machinery, and electrical engineering.

e.g. DIN 933, DIN 931, DIN 6921, DIN 934
ISO GLOBAL METRIC

International Organization for Standardization

Harmonized international guidelines governing structural properties, thread forms, and product grades.

e.g. ISO 4014, ISO 4017, ISO 4162, ISO 7040
ASTM NORTH AMERICA / HIGH TENSILE

American Society for Testing and Materials

Technical specifications for high-tensile structural bolts, heavy industrial bolting, and petrochemical fasteners.

e.g. ASTM A193, A325, A490, A307
JIS JAPAN / ASIA-PACIFIC

Japanese Industrial Standards

Specialized specifications common across Japanese automotive platforms, factory robotics, and precision instruments.

e.g. JIS B 1180, JIS B 1181, JIS B 1186
BS BRITISH / COMMONWEALTH

British Standards

Established fastener dimensions and mechanical properties referenced throughout heavy construction and fabrication.

e.g. BS 3692, BS 4190, BS 4395
CUSTOM TAILORED SPECS

Customer-Specific Requirements

Custom blueprints, specialized plating specifications (Zinc, Cadmium, Black Oxide, HDG), and specialized head designs.

Per Drawing / Sample Specification

Note: Product requirements reference applicable standards based on manufacturer specifications. Compliance is verified per individual order batch and documentation availability. We do not claim all products comply with every standard.
